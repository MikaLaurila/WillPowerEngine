# Sound attribution

`sounds/blip.wav` (the voice blip), `sounds/quest.wav` (the quest jingle) and `music/village.wav` (an
8-second loop: C, Am, F, G with a pad, bass and plucked arpeggio) were synthesized for this template by a
seeded script (sine tones with simple envelopes; 22.05 kHz, and 11.025 kHz for the music; 16-bit mono).
They are part of WillPowerEngine and covered by its MIT license. Replace them with your own.
